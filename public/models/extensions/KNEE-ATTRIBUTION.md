Adaptations: evaluation of the authored visible meniscus subdivision modifiers, triangulation, measured knee-specific uniform scale/rotation/translation into Human Atlas coordinates, correction of reflected-object winding, recomputed angle-weighted normals quantized to signed 16-bit, and binary/gzip packing. No anatomical path edit, mesh decimation, local warping, or independent displacement of a ligament/meniscus is applied. The technical QA images compare the selected source meshes and reference bones with the existing atlas bone surfaces and exported meshes.
