# Z-Anatomy sciatic nerve extension

Selected source objects: `Sciatic nerve.l` and `Sciatic nerve.r` from `Z-Anatomy/Startup.blend` in the [Z-Anatomy source archive](https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy/blob/master/Z-Anatomy.zip). Each selected object includes three authored Bezier splines. Separate tibial and common fibular nerve objects and their distal branches are excluded.

Adaptations: curve-to-triangle conversion retaining all original splines, bevel settings, control-point radii, and open ends; independently measured uniform scale/rotation/translation from hip/thigh references into Human Atlas coordinates; reflected-object winding correction; recomputed angle-weighted normals quantized to signed 16-bit; binary/gzip packing. No anatomical path edit, curve redrawing, decimation, caps, local warping or per-structure displacement was applied. Technical QA images compare the source structures with existing Human Atlas bones/muscles and exported nerve surfaces.
