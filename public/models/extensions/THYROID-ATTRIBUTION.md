Adaptations: evaluated the original Mirror, Solidify and level-1 viewport Subdivision modifiers; triangulated; applied a measured neck-specific similarity registration; recomputed normals and quantized to signed 16-bit; packed as binary and gzip. No anatomical sculpting, added shape detail, local warp or decimation was performed.

The original cage has only 84 vertices and 68 polygons. Its evaluated 1,302 vertices and 2,600 triangles improve display smoothness without adding anatomical evidence. The output is a stylized, low-detail source reference; expert review of morphology and placement is pending. Parathyroids and segmented thyroid subdivisions are not included.
