The uterus v1.2 metadata itself uses v1.1 in its citation title; that source wording is preserved above. The selected digital-object version is v1.2.

Adaptations: retain native glTF metre/Y-up positions, original source triangles and identity node transforms; pack positions into Float32, normals into Int16 and indices into Uint32; gzip compression. Source normals are normalized. Two near-zero normals in the source anterior uterine wall are derived from incident triangle normals, with a nearest-valid-source-normal fallback for degenerate incident triangles. No geometric scaling, fitting, mirroring, decimation, smoothing, closure repair or synthetic organ was introduced.

Review render colors and translucent bone context were added for visual inspection. Original GLBs and source metadata remain unmodified. This is a separate female reference assembly with four independently versioned HRA objects; metadata does not establish individual donor identity or a single-donor assembly. No complete female body or clinical accuracy is claimed.
