# Small source-use decision record

Copy this block into the existing owning intake, review, target or design record for a material choice. Omit genuinely inapplicable fields with a brief reason; group related targets when their evidence and constraints are shared. This is a decision aid, not a new approval gate. See [routing and review](agent-workflow.md#source-use-for-anatomy-and-learning-decisions).

```markdown
### Decision: <bounded claim or behavior> — <date>

- Scope/status: <approved task; decision made or proposal only; affected target/source IDs>
- Evidence: <URL/repository path + version/date + exact section/object/locator; what it supports>
- Evidence boundary: <observed / source-stated / inferred / proposed / untested / unknown; what it does not establish>
- Choice and reason: <why this source/behavior; relevant alternative or why deviating from research guidance>
- Rights/frame constraints: <applicable component license/exception and attribution; specimen, units/axes/shared transform; unresolved facts or not applicable>
- Owning change/check path: <authored input → existing producer if needed → smallest relevant checks; browser journey if visible>
- Open verification: <specific remaining source, geometry, anatomy, device or rights check; expert status>
- Review/result: <independent review findings; checks actually run and revision; observed behavior or explicitly not exercised>
```

Do not turn a published source into expert acceptance, a missing catalog entry into geometric absence, or an implemented mechanism into demonstrated learning efficacy. Leave future mechanism choices unselected until the task authorizes them. Never include credentials, machine-local paths or private conversation notes.
