Included actual source objects: FJ6044, FJ6045, FJ6046, FJ6047, FJ6049, FJ6050 and FJ6051. All returned headers identify Compatibility version 4.3, FMA 3.0 part_of, FMA8620, Right anterior segmental artery. Original source archive and per-OBJ hashes are retained.

Atlas preparation only converts source millimeters/Z-up to the established Atlas meters/Y-up frame, packs positions/indices, and computes normals averaged at identical source positions. No positional smoothing, fitting, reconstruction, anatomical redrawing or new triangles were introduced.

This is a withheld alternative parent-concept representation. Each source surface substantially overlaps an existing named pulmonary branch surface in the base Atlas. Do not add these seven on top of the existing seven surfaces; the result would duplicate representation. The package does not relabel or move the two legacy outliers, does not alter raw memberships, and does not identify their exact renal branch. Anatomical expert acceptance is pending.
