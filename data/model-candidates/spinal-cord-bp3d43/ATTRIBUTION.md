The original source OBJ files, source ZIP, mapping ZIP and license-page snapshot are retained in this folder. The neural tissue surface is FJ4426 / FMA242005. The existing atlas central canal corresponds to FJ1737 and is retained here only for source comparison and coordinate verification.

Changes in the display derivative: source millimeter coordinates converted to the existing atlas meter axes; seven zero-area source triangles and five unused vertices omitted; source normals rotated and normalized; positions/normals/indices encoded into the atlas binary layout and compressed. No anatomical reshaping, fitted deformation, smoothing or decimation was applied. Two repeated source faces and small source topology defects remain documented.
