The archive supplies no individual author/source lineage for these muscle objects. The separately attributed noncommercial inner-ear and kidney components are not exported here.

Changes: selected existing faces by authored material, applied source world transforms, corrected mirrored winding, exported OBJ. No smoothing, landmark generation, atlas registration or new anatomical surface creation. These are insufficient-source review artifacts, not verified humeral insertion footprints.
