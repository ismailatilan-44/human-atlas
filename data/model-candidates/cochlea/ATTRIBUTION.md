# Bilateral cochlea: separate noncommercial candidate

Geometry: `Cochlea.l` and `Cochlea.r`, extracted from the Z-Anatomy `Startup.blend` archive; Z-Anatomy contributors including Gauthier Kervyn and Marcin Zielinski. Z-Anatomy generally declares CC BY-SA 4.0, but its attribution file separately identifies the University of Dundee School of Medicine's **Anatomy of the Inner Ear** as referenced/included/adapted under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**.

This candidate conservatively retains that narrower **noncommercial, attribution and share-alike** scope for these two inner-ear objects. It is not a commercial-use clearance. The source objects do not contain individual author/license properties; the connection to the Dundee component is an anatomical-component/provenance inference, not a recovered per-object license certificate.

Dundee credits Annie Campbell (medical artist), Dr Patrick Spielmann (content expert), and Dr Penny Lockwood and Dr David Stewart (reviewers). Dundee's work derives from **3D Ear**, by W. Robert J. Funnell, Sam Daniel and Daren Nicolson at McGill University, under CC BY-NC-SA 1.0; Dundee identifies its derivative as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Changes: authored viewport subdivision evaluated, world transforms applied, mirrored winding corrected, one uniform cranial similarity registration applied, smooth normals calculated, and the result encoded as an atlas binary/gzip. No local deformation, additional smoothing/subdivision, or anatomical remodeling was performed. The two source-side surfaces are preserved; bilateral presence does not imply two independent specimens.

Vestibule and semicircular-canal context appears only in review renders and is not included in the candidate binary. Keep this attribution and noncommercial classification with the candidate.
